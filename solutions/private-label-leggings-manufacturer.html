FAQ

Private-label leggings questions

Can Shanlinyang make leggings from photos?

Yes. Reference photos, product links, sketches or samples are enough to begin feasibility review. A tech pack improves accuracy when measurements and grading are already decided.

What is the MOQ for private-label leggings?

Custom leggings usually start from about 100 pieces per color per style, depending on fabric, logo method, trims and packaging. Stock-based tests may support smaller quantities.

Can you help make leggings squat-proof?

Opacity depends on fabric weight, color, stretch level and construction. We recommend sample review and bulk fabric checks before production approval.

Can you support US/EU delivery?

Yes. Delivery planning can include DDP options, Amazon FBA preparation, barcode labels, carton marks, size/color separation and 3PL warehouse delivery requirements.
