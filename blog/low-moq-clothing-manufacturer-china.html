How to Evaluate a Low MOQ Clothing Manufacturer in China

PUBLISHED 2026-06-01 · UPDATED 2026-11-02 · SHANLINYANG APPAREL FACTORY TEAM · ~9 MIN READ

A low MOQ clothing manufacturer should explain the minimum by style, color, fabric and process—not advertise one number without conditions. Compare the MOQ basis, sample route, material assumptions, unit-price tiers, quality plan and payment milestones before choosing a supplier.

If you are still deciding between print-on-demand, stock wholesale and custom production, first read the no-minimum-order guide. This page focuses on custom small-batch manufacturing.

What “low MOQ” actually means

MOQ is not a single factory-wide number. Sewing efficiency, fabric supplier minimums, dye-lot size, printing or embroidery setup, trims and packaging can each create a separate threshold. A supplier may accept 100 units of one style but require those units to use one stock fabric and one or two colors.

MOQ layerQuestion to askCommon way to reduce it
Style/sewing MOQMinimum units per pattern or style?Use fewer styles and shared construction
Color MOQMinimum per color or dye lot?Choose stock colors or fewer colorways
Fabric MOQMinimum meters and usable width?Use stock or shared fabric across styles
Process MOQMinimum for print, embroidery or wash?Reuse artwork method and limit placements
Trim/packaging MOQMinimum for labels, hardware or custom bags?Use standard trims or order reusable stock

Ask for the MOQ in writing

Request the minimum per style, per color and for the full order. Include the planned size breakdown because a factory may accept a small total but require practical cutting quantities in each size. Ask what surcharge or material change applies below the standard tier.

For Shanlinyang projects, custom production may start around 100 pieces per color per style for suitable products and materials, while stock test orders may start lower. This is a planning point, not a universal promise: the confirmed quotation should state the exact minimum and assumptions for your style.

Why low-volume unit prices are higher

Pattern preparation, sampling, cutting setup, screen or embroidery setup, fabric handling and packing administration occur even when the order is small. Those costs are spread across fewer units. Small-lot fabric or custom-dye charges can add another premium.

Instead of applying a fixed percentage to every category, request prices at two or three realistic quantities. Use the same specification, color count, size range, packaging and Incoterm for every tier. The clothing factory RFQ checklist shows the fields to include.

Low MOQ supplier verification checklist

  1. Confirm capability: ask which processes are handled in-house and which are subcontracted.
  2. Verify the business: review the registered company information, production address and recent factory evidence; arrange a live video review or third-party audit when risk justifies it.
  3. Review comparable work: request examples in a similar fabric, construction and branding method rather than unrelated products.
  4. Approve a sample: define the sample type, fee, revision terms, courier charge and approval standard before bulk production.
  5. Define quality: agree measurements, tolerances, construction, materials, testing and the inspection method in writing.
  6. Define payment milestones: do not assume one standard split. Link payments to written milestones and use a method appropriate to the relationship and order risk.
  7. Define delivery: state the Incoterm and named place, lead-time starting point and responsibilities for export, freight, import and destination charges.

Red flags in a low MOQ quotation

  • A firm-looking price without fabric, quantity, color, branding or packaging assumptions.
  • One MOQ number that ignores separate fabric, color and decoration minimums.
  • Pressure to begin bulk production before a sample or specification is approved.
  • Payment instructions to an unrelated account without a documented explanation.
  • No written process for revisions, delays, inspection findings or non-conforming goods.
  • A schedule that does not identify whether timing starts at deposit, material approval or PPS approval.

Plan a first order that the factory can execute

Concentrate the first run on a few proven styles, shared materials and limited colors. This reduces setup and makes inspection easier. Keep enough budget and calendar room for sampling, one revision when needed, material confirmation, production, inspection and transport.

Before paying for bulk, follow the sample ordering checklist and use the PPS approval process. Keep the approved documents and sample references aligned with the purchase order.

Low MOQ Clothing Manufacturing FAQ

What counts as a low MOQ in China?

There is no universal number. Some custom programs begin around 50–100 units per style or color, while special fabrics, colors and processes may require more.

Is MOQ per style or per color?

It can be both. Confirm the minimum by style, color and size breakdown, plus separate material or process minimums.

Why does low MOQ cost more?

Development and setup costs are spread across fewer units, and material suppliers may charge small-lot fees or limit choices to stock options.

How can I reduce MOQ?

Use shared stock fabrics, fewer colors, standard trims, fewer styles and a consistent size range.

What should I verify before paying?

Verify capability and business details, approve a sample, and confirm the specification, MOQ basis, payment milestones, quality plan, delivery term and change process.
