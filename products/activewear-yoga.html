BUYER QUESTIONS — ACTIVEWEAR & YOGA WEAR

FAQ

Are your leggings squat-proof?
Fabric opacity depends on GSM, color, stretch level and construction. We recommend sample review before bulk production so buyers can check opacity, recovery, waistband stability and seam comfort.
Can you make private-label yoga clothing?
Yes. We can help with yoga leggings, yoga bras, tops and matching sets. For a focused route, see the private-label yoga clothing manufacturer page.
Can you add my logo and packaging?
Yes. Options include heat-transfer logos, silicone or reflective printing, woven labels, care labels, hangtags, branded poly bags, barcode labels and carton marks.
Do you ship activewear to the USA and Europe?
Yes. We support air, sea and DDP options, plus Amazon FBA and 3PL delivery planning for North American and European buyers.
