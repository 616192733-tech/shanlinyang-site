How to Get a Clothing Factory Quote: RFQ Checklist and Template

PUBLISHED 2026-07-06 · UPDATED 2026-11-02 · SHANLINYANG APPAREL FACTORY TEAM · ~9 MIN READ

To get a useful clothing factory quote, send one RFQ that defines the product, quantity, materials, construction, branding, packaging, delivery destination and requested trade term. Ask the factory to list assumptions, exclusions, price validity, sampling cost and lead time so competing quotes can be compared on the same basis.

A low number is not automatically a better quote. If one supplier prices a basic stock fabric under EXW terms and another includes custom fabric, labels, export delivery and packaging, the totals describe different orders. Start with a complete clothing sourcing brief, then use the checklist below to make each quotation comparable.

Tech pack, reference sample or photos?

A tech pack is the clearest basis for a firm quote, but it is not the only way to begin. A factory may give a planning range from a reference garment or annotated photos when you also provide measurements, materials, branding and quantity. Label that range as indicative and ask what information is still required for a firm production price.

Do not describe a material only as “soft,” “premium” or “high quality.” State measurable requirements where possible, such as composition, approximate GSM, stretch direction, finish and color reference. If the exact material is undecided, ask the supplier to quote named alternatives rather than silently choosing one.

Request tiered prices with the assumptions attached

Ask for two or three quantities that you could realistically order—for example, 100, 300 and 500 units per style—then define how those units are divided across colors and sizes. A quote for 300 units in one color is not equivalent to 300 units split across six colors.

For every tier, confirm MOQ, color minimum, currency and whether taxes, samples, labels, packaging, testing and freight are included. Also request the quotation validity period because fabric, trim, freight and exchange-rate inputs can change.

Specify the Incoterm and named place

“FOB price” is incomplete without a named port, and it is not the right basis for every shipment. Ask the supplier or freight partner which term fits the transport plan, then write the term and named place—for example, FCA [named place], FOB [named port] for appropriate sea or inland-waterway shipments, or DAP [named destination]. Review the clothing price and Incoterms guide before comparing landed cost.

Separately confirm import duties, taxes, customs clearance and destination charges. A factory unit price should not be treated as the final landed cost unless the quotation explicitly defines those responsibilities.

Compare the full quotation, not only unit price

  • Specification basis: exact fabric, construction, trims, branding and packaging quoted.
  • Commercial basis: currency, Incoterm, named place, MOQ, price tier and validity date.
  • Development: sample type, fee, courier charge, revision terms and estimated schedule.
  • Production: lead-time starting point, capacity assumptions and approval dependencies.
  • Quality: tolerance, inspection plan, testing and process for non-conforming goods.
  • Payment: deposit, balance trigger, payment method and any bank charges.
  • Exclusions: anything the factory expects the buyer or a third party to supply or pay.

If a price is unusually low, ask which item or service is different. The answer may be a lighter fabric, stock color, simpler seam, excluded packaging or a different delivery basis—not necessarily better purchasing.

Copy-and-edit clothing factory inquiry template

Subject: RFQ — [Product] — [Quantity] — [Brand]

Hello [Factory Name],

We are sourcing [product/category] for [brand and market]. Please quote the attached specification or reference based on the details below.

  • Styles: [style names or numbers]
  • Quantity: [units per style/color; requested price tiers]
  • Sizes: [range and breakdown if available]
  • Fabric: [composition, GSM, stretch, finish, color]
  • Branding: [labels, print, embroidery, hangtags]
  • Packaging: [polybag, barcode, carton requirements]
  • Delivery: [destination and requested Incoterm/named place]
  • Schedule: [sample approval and delivery target]
  • Testing: [required tests or market requirements]

Please state the MOQ, unit price by quantity, currency, quote validity, sample fee and timing, bulk lead time, payment terms, included items, exclusions and any assumptions or alternative materials used.

Regards,
[Name]
[Brand]
[Website or sales channel]

After the quote: verify with a sample

Before approving bulk production, confirm how the quote changes if the sample requires different fabric, construction or trims. Follow the clothing sample ordering checklist and document final approval using the PPS approval process.

For a factory quotation, send your style reference, quantity and destination. If details are incomplete, clearly label the reply as a planning range and list what is still needed for confirmation.

Clothing Factory Quote FAQ

What information should an RFQ include?

Include product references, construction, fabric and trims, branding, sizes, quantity by style and color, packaging, destination, Incoterm, schedule and testing needs.

Can I request a quote without a tech pack?

Yes. Clear photos, a reference garment and written specifications may support a planning range. A firm production quote requires enough detail to define materials, construction, measurements, branding and quantity.

Why request tiered pricing?

Several realistic quantities show how material minimums and setup costs change the unit price. Ask the factory to state the color and size assumptions behind every tier.

What should I compare besides price?

Compare the specification, currency, Incoterm, validity, MOQ, sample charges, inclusions, payment terms, lead time, quality standard and exclusions.

Is the first quote final?

It may be indicative when details remain open. Ask what can change the price and when it will be reconfirmed after material or sample approval.
