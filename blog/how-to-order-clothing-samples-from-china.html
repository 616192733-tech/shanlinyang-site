Shipping Clothing Samples from China: Cost, Courier and Order Checklist

PUBLISHED 2026-06-05 · UPDATED 2026-11-12 · SHANLINYANG APPAREL FACTORY TEAM · ~8 MIN READ

To order and ship a clothing sample from China, send a clear tech pack or reference, confirm the sample fee and completion date, then choose an express courier, verify the delivery address, customs description, declared value and who pays duties before dispatch.

Start with a complete sourcing brief. After the sample arrives, use the checks below and record approvals for the sample-to-bulk process. A sample is a development tool, not a guarantee that unspecified details will automatically carry into production.

Sample types and what they're for

SamplePurposePlanning range / terms to confirm
Stock sampleJudge sewing quality & fabric of an existing styleUnit price (from 2 pcs)
Counter sampleFactory develops from your reference garment, tech pack or photosOften USD 30–80; confirm whether any credit applies to bulk
Pre-production (PP) sampleFinal sign-off in approved bulk fabric and trims before cuttingConfirm whether included, separately charged or credited

Realistic timeline

A straightforward counter or PP sample may take about 7–12 business days after the specification and materials are confirmed. Custom fabric, trims, pattern development and revision rounds can extend the schedule; courier transit is additional. Ask for a dated sequence covering material confirmation, sample completion, photos, dispatch and feedback.

Shipping clothing samples from China

Most one-off garment samples travel by express courier because the shipment is small and buyers need tracking. Ask the factory for the packed weight and carton size, then compare DHL, FedEx, UPS or another available courier to your destination. The cheapest quote is not always the lowest landed cost: confirm whether fuel surcharge, remote-area delivery, customs brokerage, import duty and tax are included or collected from the receiver.

Do not ask a supplier to mark a false value or inaccurate description. Customs treatment varies by destination and sample status, so the buyer should check local import rules or ask the courier before shipment. If the buyer has a courier account, confirm in writing whether the factory should ship collect on that account or prepay freight and add it to the invoice.

Shipping timeline to plan

Keep three dates separate: sample completion, courier pickup and delivery. Express transit may be only a few business days on some routes, but weekends, remote areas, weather and customs clearance can add time. Build approval time after delivery into the production calendar instead of treating the courier estimate as a guaranteed arrival date.

The arrival checklist

  • Measure before you try on. Check key points against the spec sheet: bust, waist, hip, length, sleeve. Note deviations in cm.
  • Inspect construction. Check crotch, underarm and waistband seams, stitch consistency, skipped stitches, seam stretch and any construction requirements in the tech pack.
  • Wash one sample. One wash cycle reveals shrinkage, twisting and print durability before you own 300 units of the problem.
  • Photograph everything. Front, back, interior, labels — your comments thread becomes the production reference.

Writing fit comments factories can act on

"It feels off" can't be sewn. "Raise the front rise 1.5 cm; take the waist in 2 cm; keep hip as is" can. Comment in measurements against specific points, one line per change, and confirm what should NOT change — pattern rooms act on deltas, not vibes.

Approve the sample before bulk production

Send one consolidated comment sheet with annotated photos and measurements. Mark every point as approved, revise or not applicable, then ask the factory to confirm the changes in writing. Keep the approved sample, measurement chart, fabric, trims, labels, logo method and packaging as the bulk-production reference.

Review the quality-control process before approving production. When ready, request a stock, counter or pre-production sample quote with your reference, target quantity and destination country.

Frequently Asked Questions About Clothing Samples

How do I order a clothing sample from China?

Send a tech pack, reference garment or clear photos with measurements, fabric direction, color, branding, size and target quantity. Confirm the sample type, fee, courier charge, completion date and revision policy before paying.

How long does a clothing sample take?

A straightforward sample may take about 7–12 business days after specifications and materials are confirmed. Custom materials and revisions add time; courier transit is additional.

What should I inspect?

Check measurements, fit, fabric, color, construction, seams, trims, labels, logo placement, wash performance and packaging against the approved specification.

Are sample fees refundable?

Policies vary. Some factories credit a sample fee against a qualifying bulk order, but the amount and conditions should be written in the quotation.

How do I ship clothing samples from China?

Compare the packed weight and dimensions with the available express couriers. Confirm the service, price, customs paperwork, importer charges, delivery contact details and tracking number before dispatch.

Who pays customs charges on samples?

The quotation should state whether freight is prepaid or collect. The receiver may still owe destination duty, tax or brokerage, depending on local rules and the shipment.
